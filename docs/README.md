# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation hub. This repository centralizes the core practices, workflows, and responsibilities used to plan, execute, monitor, and improve cross-functional work across the organization.

## Our Approach

OctoAcme follows a structured but flexible project management approach grounded in five core principles:

- Customer-first: prioritize customer value and usability
- Iterative delivery: deliver small, testable increments
- Clear ownership: each initiative has defined leaders and responsibilities
- Data-informed decisions: measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- Psychological safety: encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## Brief Process Summary

OctoAcme projects typically move through a repeatable lifecycle:

- Initiation: confirm the business problem, stakeholders, success metrics, and go/no-go decision
- Planning: define scope, backlog, roles, dependencies, milestones, and acceptance criteria
- Execution: run the work, track progress, manage blockers, and maintain quality
- Release: validate readiness, deploy safely, and communicate outcomes
- Retrospective: review performance, document learnings, and identify improvements

This approach helps teams balance speed with quality, visibility, and alignment across stakeholders.
