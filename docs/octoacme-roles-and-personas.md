# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owners

### Role Summary
Product Owners are accountable for defining and prioritizing the value delivered by the project. They bridge business needs with product direction and work closely with stakeholders, PMs, and delivery teams to ensure the roadmap stays aligned with impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the product vision and priorities
- Translate stakeholder needs into actionable backlog items
- Clarify scope, business value, and acceptance criteria
- Prioritize features based on customer and business impact
- Partner with the PM to align delivery trade-offs and dependencies

### Goals
- Maximize value delivered to customers and the business
- Keep the backlog focused on the highest-priority outcomes
- Maintain clear decision-making around scope and trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Roadmap reviews and prioritization sessions
- Backlog refinement and stakeholder alignment meetings
- Acceptance criteria discussions with engineering and QA

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to sequence delivery and resolve conflicts in priorities
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to ensure work meets business intent and acceptance criteria
- Provides direction to stakeholders and aligns business expectations with delivery realities

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality strategy for the project and ensure the product meets agreed standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and quality gates
- Validate acceptance criteria, regression risk, and release readiness
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Partner with engineering and product stakeholders on defect triage
- Recommend release readiness and escalation when quality risks remain

### Goals
- Prevent high-risk defects from reaching customers
- Improve confidence in quality and release decisions
- Align testing effort with business and customer risk

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and acceptance reviews
- Defect triage and go/no-go discussions
- Release readiness and validation updates

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate that features meet acceptance criteria
- Supports Product Owners in confirming that value has been delivered
- Helps Project Managers understand delivery risk and release constraints

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning, preparation, and execution of software releases to reduce operational risk and improve stakeholder communication.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, sequencing, and rollback readiness
- Review release scope, dependencies, and rollback plans
- Communicate launch details to stakeholders and support teams
- Track deployment health and incident response readiness
- Ensure release artifacts and documentation are complete

### Goals
- Deliver changes with minimal customer disruption
- Improve release predictability and consistency
- Strengthen communication of changes, risks, and mitigations

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release checklists and deployment reviews
- Stakeholder notifications and launch updates
- Post-deploy verification and incident follow-up

### Interaction with existing roles
- Coordinates with Project Managers on scope and timing
- Works with QA Leads to confirm readiness for deployment
- Partners with Product Owners to communicate what is changing and why
- Collaborates with Engineering Leads to ensure rollback or mitigation plans are in place

---

## Security Champions

### Role Summary
Security Champions help protect the project from security and compliance risks by embedding security considerations into planning, design, and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and changes for security risks
- Validate compliance requirements and secure design patterns
- Identify vulnerabilities, data protection concerns, and controls
- Partner with engineering and release teams to reduce risk
- Escalate security issues according to project policies

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Improve secure-by-default delivery practices
- Support resilient, trusted product operations

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat discussions
- Risk escalation and mitigation planning
- Security sign-off guidance for release readiness

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Engineering Leads on secure implementation approaches
- Supports Project Managers and Release Managers with risk awareness during delivery and deployment
- Helps Product Owners understand security trade-offs and compliance implications

---

## Stakeholder Success Managers

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Success Managers maintain alignment between the project team and key business stakeholders, helping manage expectations, communication, and executive visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Track stakeholder concerns, needs, and decision points
- Coordinate status updates and feedback loops with business leaders
- Help resolve cross-functional misunderstandings and dependencies
- Ensure stakeholders understand delivery progress and risks
- Facilitate executive communication for milestones and escalations

### Goals
- Maintain stakeholder trust and alignment
- Reduce uncertainty by improving communication quality
- Support smoother decision-making across functions

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone stakeholder updates
- Decision logs and executive summaries
- Escalation briefs for business-impacting items

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to manage communication cadence and key updates
- Partners with Product Owners to ensure stakeholder expectations reflect current priorities
- Coordinates with Release Managers to communicate launch impact and outcomes

---

## Role Interaction Model

To improve accountability and delivery clarity, each role contributes to a shared operating model:

- Product Owners define value and priority
- Project Managers coordinate schedule, communication, and risk
- Developers implement and validate the work
- QA Leads validate quality and readiness
- Release Managers manage deployment risk and communication
- Security Champions reduce security and compliance exposure
- Stakeholder Success Managers maintain alignment with business stakeholders

This model makes decision ownership explicit and helps the team escalate issues at the right level while keeping alignment across product, engineering, and business stakeholders.
